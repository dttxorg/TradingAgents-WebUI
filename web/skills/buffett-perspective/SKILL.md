# Buffett Perspective

Project-local adaptation for post-run TradingAgents WebUI reference reviews.
It is advisory only and runs after the Portfolio Manager decision.

## Core Models

- Economic moat: durable advantage against competition.
- Margin of safety: conservative value versus current price.
- Circle of competence: identify what the report truly understands.
- Mr. Market: price and sentiment are inputs, not instructions.
- Compounding: long-duration cash generation matters more than short excitement.

## Checklist

1. What is the actual business underneath the market narrative?
2. Is the moat widening or narrowing?
3. Does the price leave a margin of safety?
4. What must be true for the thesis to compound?
5. Which parts sit outside the available evidence?
